What is a FAB Prepaid Card?

First, let's understand what a prepaid card is. Unlike a regular bank account, a prepaid card is not connected to a savings or current account. You can only spend the money that has been loaded onto the card. Think of it like a gift card that you can use anywhere. Many companies in the UAE use FAB prepaid cards to pay salaries. This system is called the Wage Protection Scheme (WPS), and it helps make sure everyone gets paid on time and safely.

These cards are perfect for people who might not have a full bank account. They let you buy things in shops, pay for services online, and take out cash from ATMs. Because your spending is limited to the balance on the card, it helps you control your budget easily.

How to Check FAB Card Balance (The Two-Step Process)

The official way to check your balance is through the FAB Prepaid Card Enquiry Portal. It is a simple page that is not part of the main online banking system. This means you can access it directly without signing in.

Here are the simple steps:

  1. Go to the Portal: Open your browser and go to the official FAB enquiry page (you can also use the FAB balance enquiry portal for quick access).
  2. Enter Your Details: On the page, you will see two boxes to fill in. The first box asks for the last two digits of your 16-digit card number. The second box asks for your Card ID (printed on the card, usually on the bottom-left).
  3. Click ‘Go’: After entering both, click the button that says ‘Go’ or ‘Submit’.
  4. See Your Balance: Your current balance and recent transactions appear instantly.

This portal is available 24/7, free of charge, and works from any device. Many users rely on this FAB prepaid card enquiry method because it’s fast and doesn’t require any registration.

Troubleshooting Your FAB Prepaid Card Enquiry

Sometimes, you might face a problem when you try to check your balance. Here are some common issues and how to fix them.

"Invalid Card ID" Error

This is the most common mistake. Many people confuse the Card ID with the card number. The Card ID is usually shorter and printed on the front of your card (often on the bottom left). The 16-digit card number is the long number across the middle. When doing a FAB prepaid card enquiry, make sure you are entering the correct ID, not the long number.

"Incorrect Last Two Digits"

The portal asks for the last two digits of your card number. Make sure you are entering only these two numbers. Look at your card and type them in carefully.
