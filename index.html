FAB Prepaid Card Inquiry | Complete Balance Guide

First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) issues millions of prepaid and salary cards across the UAE. Whether you hold a Ratibi salary card, a corporate prepaid card, or a government benefit card, knowing how to check your balance quickly and securely is essential.

This comprehensive guide covers every method – from the official online portal to the mobile app, ATM, SMS, and customer support. You'll also learn how to fix common errors, protect your funds, and understand your Card ID vs. card number.

Understanding the FAB Prepaid Card Ecosystem

FAB offers several prepaid products, but the most common are:

  • Ratibi Salary Card: The most widely used prepaid card for salary disbursement under the UAE Wages Protection System (WPS).
  • FAB Payment Cards: Used by businesses for petty cash and recurring payments.
  • Government Prepaid Cards: For benefits and allowances (e.g., AjmanPay Sahala).

Key distinction: A prepaid card is not a traditional bank account. You cannot log in to standard FAB online banking with your card details. Instead, you use the dedicated Prepaid Card Inquiry Portal (powered by Magnati).

Complete Inquiry Methods

We've compiled every possible way to perform a FAB prepaid card inquiry. Choose the one that fits your situation.

1. Official Online Portal (Recommended)

No registration or app download required. Just your card and a browser.

  • Go to the Magnati PPC portal (official FAB prepaid inquiry page).
  • Enter the last 4 digits of your 16-digit card number.
  • Enter your Card ID (printed separately on the card – not the 16-digit number).
  • Complete the captcha and click Go.
  • View your balance and last 10 transactions.

2. FAB Mobile App

After a one-time registration, you can check your balance, review transactions, and manage your card.

  • Download the FAB Mobile app (iOS/Android).
  • Register using your Emirates ID and card details.
  • Log in with PIN or biometrics – your balance appears on the dashboard.

3. ATM Inquiry

  • Insert your card at any FAB ATM (or other UAE ATMs, fees may apply).
  • Enter your PIN and select Balance Inquiry.
  • View the balance on screen or print a receipt.

4. SMS Banking

  • Send BAL + last 4 digits of your card number to 6667 or 2121.
  • Receive your balance via SMS (standard charges may apply).

5. Customer Support

  • Ratibi support: 600 52 2298 (UAE)
  • General prepaid support: 600 52 5500
  • Follow the IVR or speak to an agent to hear your balance.

Ratibi Salary Card & Common Issues

The Ratibi card is a payroll prepaid card for employees earning up to AED 5,000. It's part of the WPS system. Here's how to check your salary and fix problems.

How to Check Ratibi Salary Online

  • Open the prepaid card inquiry portal.
  • Enter last 4 digits of the card number + Card ID + captcha.
  • View available balance and recent transactions.
Salary not showing? Common reasons:
  • Employer hasn't submitted payroll yet.
  • Incorrect employee or card details on file.
  • Card expired or replaced.
  • WPS file rejected or holiday delay.

First check transaction history, then contact your employer's HR/payroll department.

Balance Not Updating After Purchase

Some merchants (hotels, petrol stations) place a temporary hold on your card. The available balance may be lower than the current balance until the transaction settles. Always check the transaction list for pending amounts.

Security & Best Practice

  • Only use official FAB/Magnati portals – never enter your card details on third-party sites.
  • Never share your PIN, OTP, or CVV – FAB will never ask for these.
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi when checking your balance – use mobile data.
  • Report lost/stolen cards immediately via customer support.
  • Regularly review transactions to spot unauthorized activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can I check my FAB balance without the app? Yes – use the online portal, ATM, or SMS.
  • Where is the Card ID? Printed separately on the front or back of the card. Look for a shorter number.
  • Why is the portal saying "No record found"? Usually because of incorrect Card ID/last 4 digits, or salary not yet credited.
  • Is the prepaid balance enquiry free? The online portal is free. ATM fees may apply at non-FAB machines.
  • What if the portal is not loading? Try a different browser, clear cache, disable VPN, or use mobile data.

For urgent issues, always contact FAB directly. Never share your card details with unofficial sources.
