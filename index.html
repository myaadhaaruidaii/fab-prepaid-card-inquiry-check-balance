What is a Ratibi Card?

The Ratibi Card is a prepaid salary card issued by First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) for employees in the UAE. It is part of the Wage Protection System (WPS), which ensures workers are paid on time and in full. The card is widely used for expatriate workers, especially those earning up to AED 5,000 per month, though anyone whose employer uses the Ratibi system can receive their wages on this card.

Your employer loads your salary onto the card each month. You can then withdraw cash from ATMs, pay at shops, or transfer money. The card is managed by FAB through its payment processor Magnati. Because it is a prepaid card, you can only spend what is loaded – no overdraft, no debt. This makes a ratibi fab balance check crucial to know your available funds.

Method 1: SMS – The Fastest Ratibi FAB Balance Check

The quickest way for a ratibi fab balance check is via SMS. No internet needed – works on any phone. Just open your messaging app, type BAL (or bal), and send it to 6667. Within seconds, you'll receive an SMS with your available balance. This service costs around AED 0.30 per message (standard network charges apply). Make sure your mobile number is registered with the bank, otherwise the SMS won't work. If you recently changed your number, update it with your employer or FAB.

SMS quick steps: Open messages → type BAL → send to 6667 → receive balance. Easy!

Method 2: Online Portal – Prepaid Card Enquiry

For a detailed ratibi fab balance check, use the official online portal: ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry. You don't need to log in to a bank account. Just enter the last four digits of your 16-digit card number, your Card ID (a separate number printed on the card), and the captcha. Then click “Go”. The portal will display your current balance and recent transactions. This is a free service, available 24/7. The Card ID is not the same as the main card number – check both sides of your card carefully.

If you forget your Card ID, you can find it printed on the front or back of your Ratibi card. Do not confuse it with your Emirates ID or employee number. The portal is secure and works on any browser.

Method 3: FAB Mobile App – Modern & Convenient

The FAB Mobile app is a powerful tool for a ratibi fab balance check. Download it from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Register using your card number and mobile number. Once logged in, you can see your balance, transaction history, and even get salary credit notifications. The app supports biometric login (fingerprint or face) for quick access. It also shows pending transactions and lets you manage your card on the go. This is the best method if you have a smartphone and want extra features beyond just a balance check.

Method 4: ATM – Reliable & Offline

Using an ATM for a ratibi fab balance check is a classic and reliable method. Insert your Ratibi card, enter your PIN, select “Balance Enquiry” or “Balance Inquiry”, and your balance appears on screen. You can also print a receipt. Checking balance at FAB ATMs is free; other bank ATMs may charge AED 1–2. This method works without internet and is available 24/7. Always cover the keypad when entering your PIN, and remember to take your card and receipt before leaving.

Method 5: Customer Support – When You Need Help

If none of the above methods work, or if you have a problem with your card, contact FAB support. For Ratibi card issues, call 600 52 2298 (UAE) or +971 2 499 6279 (outside UAE). For general FAB banking, call 600 52 5500. They can assist with balance checks, lost cards, or missing salary. Keep your card number and Emirates ID ready for verification.

What If Your Salary Is Not Showing?

Sometimes you perform a ratibi fab balance check and your salary is not there. Don't panic. First, wait 24–48 hours after payday – processing can take time. Second, contact your employer's HR or payroll department. They may have submitted the file late or with incorrect details. Third, check your transaction history for any pending credits. If all else fails, call FAB support. Remember, the bank cannot credit money that hasn't been sent by your employer.

Card ID vs Card Number – What's the Difference?

For an online ratibi fab balance check, you need the last four digits of your card number AND the separate Card ID. The Card ID is a unique identifier printed on your card, usually on the front or back. It is not the 16-digit payment number, nor your Emirates ID. If you can't find it, check both sides of the card carefully. If it's worn out, contact Ratibi support for help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my FAB Ratibi balance online?

Open the prepaid card enquiry portal (ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry). Enter the last four digits of your card number, the Card ID, and the captcha. Click Go to see your balance.

Is the SMS balance check free?

No, standard SMS charges apply (around AED 0.30 per message). The online portal and FAB ATMs are free.

What if I forget my Card ID?

Check both sides of your Ratibi card. The Card ID is printed separately. If you still can't find it, call Ratibi support at 600 52 2298.

Can I check my balance at any ATM?

Yes, but FAB ATMs are free. Other ATMs may charge a small fee (AED 1–2). Always check the screen for fees before proceeding.

Why does my ATM balance differ from the app?

The ATM may show available balance (after pending holds), while the app might show current balance (posted transactions). Pending payments like hotel holds can cause a temporary difference.

Security Tips for Balance Checks

  • Cover the keypad when entering your PIN at ATMs.
  • Only use official portals – never enter card details on suspicious websites.
  • Never share your PIN or OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from the bank.
  • Log out from the mobile app or online banking after checking.
  • Report lost cards immediately to FAB support.
